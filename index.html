// The In Silico Edge

Evolutionary Scaffolds.
Atomic Precision.

Natural products evolved over millions of years to interact selectively with biological systems. We pair these privileged chemical architectures with modern physics-based modeling to discover new therapeutic possibilities.

// MOLECULAR DIVERSITY

National Cancer Institute • SBIR Phase I

Bcl-2 Allostery & Resistance Dynamics

Targeted oncology therapies like venetoclax frequently encounter acquired resistance through mutations in the BH3 groove (such as G101V). Our computational campaign investigates whether rare rice-derived diol scaffolds can engage alternative back-face surfaces on Bcl-2 to bypass groove-specific resistance.

// Agricultural Ground

From Heritage Grain
To Atomic Targets.

Before a molecule can become a therapeutic lead, it needs an authentic physical origin. Rather than relying on petroleum-derived starting blocks, our discovery pipeline is anchored in the natural metabolic machinery of domestic rice bran.

In 2026, to22s Bio-Systems secured 100 pounds of foundation seed of the USDA-Tiara purple rice lineage, transferred from the USDA Agricultural Research Service (Dale Bumpers National Rice Research Center in Stuttgart, Arkansas). Tiara is a high-yield genetic outlier renowned for deep anthocyanin pigmentation and enriched triterpene profiles.
